# MarketReader — product pitch

Most watchlists show a price you already knew. MarketReader shows what changed since
you last looked. The window is your own watermark, not a fixed 1D, and it
adapts: ten minutes replays ticks, a month gives net move, worst drawdown and the
session that caused it. Meaningful is volatility-normalised and market-relative,
so a 3% day is 3.7 sigma in HDFC Bank and 1.9 in Suzlon — news in one, a Tuesday
in the other. Corporate actions and broken feeds stay out of price news: Nestlé's
10:1 reads −89.8% naively, +1.6% for the holder. Everything held back is listed,
with reasons.
